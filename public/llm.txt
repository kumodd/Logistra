# Logistra

> Logistra helps growing D2C brands position best-selling inventory closer to demand for faster delivery, better customer experience, and approximately similar delivery cost.

## About

Logistra is an India-focused e-commerce fulfilment partner for growing D2C brands, marketplace sellers, and regional businesses going online. Logistra operates the physical and digital workflow behind an order: receiving, storage, picking, packing, dispatch, tracking, returns, RTO, and reconciliation.

The core operating model is simple: a brand forwards orders to Logistra, and Logistra fulfils them from selected inventory positioned closer to customers where appropriate. Through shared fulfilment infrastructure, brands can assess a regional operation without immediately building a standalone warehouse. Each brand’s inventory, SKUs, and orders remain identified and controlled within the agreed operating model.

## Important qualification

Delivery timelines, distances, and shipping costs vary by product, weight, destination, carrier, and order mix. Any 7–9 day, 2–4 day, 1,500–2,500+ km, 200–700 km, or rupee-cost comparison on the website is a theoretical illustrative example, not a guaranteed Logistra performance claim.

## Core services

- Inbound receiving
- SKU-level storage and inventory control
- Pick and pack
- Dispatch and shipment tracking
- Returns and RTO processing
- COD reconciliation
- Kitting and value-added services

## Primary audience

- Growing D2C brands
- Marketplace sellers managing multiple sales channels
- Established brands exploring expansion into Eastern India
- Regional businesses moving online
- Businesses assessing a regional fulfilment model based on their demand geography

## Commercial clarity

Logistra does not publish a public rate card on this website. A commercial estimate depends on product dimensions and weight, order profile, inventory requirements, destination mix, receiving, storage, pick and pack, packaging, outbound shipping, returns, inventory transfers, and selected services. Shorter outbound distance does not automatically guarantee a lower total fulfilment cost.

## Shared fulfilment

Multiple brands may use common fulfilment infrastructure and operational resources. Shared infrastructure does not mean shared inventory: brand stock, SKUs, and orders remain appropriately identified and controlled within the agreed process.

## Assessment and serviceability

Logistra is based in Patna, Bihar, India, with a focus on Eastern India. Facility and carrier availability, delivery timelines, order handoff methods, reporting, integrations, and service scope require confirmation for each proposed operating model.
